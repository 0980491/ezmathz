how it works

Press any key to reveal the first problem; that starts the clock. Type the answer: it is accepted the moment it is right, so there is no enter key. A wrong digit turns red; backspace and fix it, or press enter to skip.

Pick operations, digits and a length in the bar above the problem. time counts down (15–120 s), problems ends after a fixed number, zen runs until you finish it with shift + enter.

After a test, review drills the problems you skipped, got wrong, or were slowest at.

ppm is problems solved per minute. acc is the share of keystrokes that were not mistakes. consistency is how even your solve times were.
